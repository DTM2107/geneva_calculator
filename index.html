Take it to CAD or the laser

Copy the outlines as an SVG at true size in your chosen unit. It holds the wheel, the lock disc and the driver plate with its pin hole. Arcs are drawn as short line segments.

How it is sized. Crank radius a = C·sin(π/n). The pin first touches a slot at distance b = C·cos(π/n) from the wheel centre. The wheel rim sits a margin m beyond b, and the lock disc and notch are sized so the disc clears the wheel tips exactly when the pin enters and leaves. Values are theoretical, so round to what your process can hold.
